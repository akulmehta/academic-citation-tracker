What is deliberately not counted

Citing works Google Scholar lists twice

A preprint, repository copy, author upload, thesis or database copy of a paper that is already on the same citing page. The packet counts the work once, under the row shown in Counted once as. None of these rows is highlighted on the exhibit PDF: per the decision of 2026-11-05, a row that does not stand for its work is printed unmarked, so the yellow marks on the pages and the counted citations are the same figure.
