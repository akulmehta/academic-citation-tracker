The literature that cites two of these papers together

Co-citation network

Each circle is one of the PI’s publications, numbered, sized by its independent citations and coloured by research programme: red for the anticoagulant and thrombosis work, blue for glycoscience, purple where a paper genuinely belongs to both, and green for the medicinal-chemistry papers that belong to neither. Within a programme the shade deepens for the earlier publication. A line joins two publications that the same independent citing paper cites, and its thickness is how many papers cite both — so the picture is not a metaphor for connection, it is a count. That the red and blue groups are joined at all is the point: the literature itself does not keep the two programmes apart.

Which node is which publication

Node numbers are assigned once, by research programme and then by citation count, and are identical on the citation wheel, so a number quoted from one figure means the same publication on the other. The area programme is recorded in tools/topics.js and is a classification of the publication, not a count: no figure in the packet depends on it.
