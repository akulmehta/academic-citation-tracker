8–9

The working pages

Two pages hold the rows that are not simply counted. They are kept apart on purpose. One is the record of citations that no rule could settle and a person had to decide; the other is a disclosure of citations withheld for language, on which no decision is being sought. Do not mix them: a decision on a row from the second cannot change a figure in the first.

8 Open rows, settled The citations no rule could settle, grouped by what made them hard: contradicted by an external record, cleared externally but truncated on Scholar, or not indexed anywhere. Every one has been decided, so the buckets are empty on this snapshot; the chapter keeps the shape of what was checked, and each decision carries its row_uid, the record it cites and a link to the exhibit page it sits on. Rows flagged non-English were never offered here — see chapter 9. 9 Non-English rows Every row flagged as possibly not listed in English, and what the packet gives up by excluding it. No decision is recorded here and no column invites one.
