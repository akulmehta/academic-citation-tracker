Indep. is the countable independent figure: a citing work that shares no author with the paper it cites, is settled by corroboration or a complete Google Scholar author list, and is not withheld for language. It is what the exhibit PDFs mark in yellow. Distinct collapses the duplicate versions Scholar shows of the same paper. Unsettled includes the rows withheld for language, so adding the two would count them twice. On paper the venue and status columns are dropped so the remaining seventeen fit the sheet width; both appear here on screen and in dashboard/data/citation-data.json.
