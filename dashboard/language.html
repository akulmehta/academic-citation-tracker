Nothing here needs a decision

Per the project guide, a citation not listed in English cannot be highlighted and cannot count toward the adjusted total without a human translator fluent in both languages and an accompanying certification or affidavit. Machine translation is explicitly not certified. On 2026-11-01 the decision was taken not to obtain translations, so these entries stay unhighlighted and the packet reports an adjusted independent total based solely on English citations — the guide's second path.

Resolving one of these rows would not change any figure while that decision stands. That is why they are on a page of their own rather than with the citations that needed a person to decide them, and why this table has no decision column.

Why each row was held back

The screen keys on the title, because the guide defines an entry as the title of the article, the list of authors and the venue — not the body. A foreign-language abstract under an English title is therefore not evidence of a non-English entry, and Greek letters in a title are read as scientific notation (an alpha-1,2-mannosidase or beta-catenin title is English) while they stay a clear minority of the title's letters. A non-Latin script elsewhere in the entry flags it only where that script is the bulk of the text, so a foreign abstract still counts against the entry while one mis-rendered character in an English sentence does not. The flag is a conservative screen, not a ruling.

Two rows were recovered on 2026-11-02 when both tests became shares rather than absolute counts: cit-000062, an English title carrying six alpha and beta symbols, and cit-000075, an English title whose abstract renders one ring-bond descriptor as a Cyrillic letter. The rest of this table is unchanged — what remains here is genuinely not listed in English.

If translations were ever obtained, the figures these rows would recover are the Would count rows below, and the adjusted English-only total would be replaced rather than reported alongside. Until then this page is disclosure, not work.
