Why the current screen misses foreign titles. capture-citations.js tests three
things: a non-Latin script anywhere in title or snippet; two or more function
words English does not use; and two or more distinct accented letters in the
TITLE. The accented range is Latin-1 Supplement only, so Polish l a e s,
Czech, Croatian and Turkish letters (Latin Extended-A/B) are invisible. The
word list has no Polish, Croatian or Czech forms. Separately, the Greek-
notation exemption vetoes the whole non-Latin test, so a CJK title containing a
Greek letter such as the alpha in GPIba skips it entirely.

     Literal reading of the instruction. Because the snippet stops mattering,
     it also UN-SUPPRESSES rows flagged today only by their snippet, so A adds
     marks as well as removing them.
  B  TITLE decides, but a non-Latin SCRIPT anywhere still flags. CJK, Cyrillic,
     Arabic and the rest cannot be scientific notation, so keeping them costs
     no false positives; only Greek-based and word-based snippet flags are
     dropped.
  C  the currently stored flag OR the new title flag. Purely subtractive:
     removes marks, never adds one.
