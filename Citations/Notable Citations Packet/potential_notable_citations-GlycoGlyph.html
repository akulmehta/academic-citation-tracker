Notes on the list

“Open copy” is a statement about access, not about the paper. Open copy found means a downloadable version exists and, where the PDF column has a link, it is already in this folder. Paywalled means the article is real and the publisher gates the file — an access the applicant’s own subscription or an interlibrary loan can settle. No open copy and Not in OpenAlex mean the look-up completed and found nothing downloadable; they are not findings that the paper does not exist, and a publisher page or a library copy can still supply the text.

Independence was re-checked against the citation record, not the queued snapshot. The record reads the complete author list of both papers. A citing paper that shares an author with the cited work, or that names Richard D. Cummings or the former PI Umesh R. Desai, is a self-citation and was removed at the first screen above.

A tier is a scan result, and only for an article that has actually been read. The Tier and Citation columns are filled from the PDF on file: scan-mentions.js extracts the text, finds every appearance of the tool, and reads the heading the article itself printed above it. Tier A means the mention sits in the Methods as the method performed; B in the Results or Figures; C in a high-reputation publication; D an acknowledgement. Where the column says no tier, the article was read and the tool appears only in a reference list, a table, or a section that earns no tier — the note says which. Where it says not read, no PDF exists yet, so no claim is made. A scan is a machine reading of prose: it is good at telling a Methods sentence from a bibliography entry, and it cannot tell whether the use was substantial. A person’s reading of the page decides, and filing a candidate runs the same scanner again into the packet.

A blue “Tier X — resolved” pill means the citing paper never names the tool.
